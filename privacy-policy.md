Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SYNTXRR STREAM handles information when you use the application.

SYNTXRR STREAM is an Android streaming companion and streaming studio application.

2. Information and Permissions

Depending on the features you use, the application may process:

- Account information: information provided through supported account-linking features.
- Streaming configuration: overlay settings, preferences, and song request settings.
- Local media: audio or other media files you choose to import.
- Camera and microphone: data accessed when you activate related features.
- Android permissions: permissions required for notifications, floating overlays, audio, and other application functions.
- Technical information: diagnostic information needed to identify errors and improve reliability, where enabled.

3. TikTok Integration

When TikTok integration is available and enabled, the application may use authorized TikTok authentication and supported account information.

Authentication and access to TikTok services are subject to TikTok's own permissions, policies, and approval requirements.

SYNTXRR STREAM does not claim access to private TikTok information or unauthorized data from other applications.

4. Floating Companion and Event Data

Companion features are designed to process only information made available through authorized event sources.

Temporary LIVE event information may be processed in memory to support chat, gifts, follows, and related companion features. Such temporary event data is intended to be limited and is not intended to become a permanent chat-history archive.

The application is not designed to record your screen or read private data from other applications.

5. Local Storage

Some settings and imported media may be stored locally on your Android device. You can manage application data through Android settings.

6. Sharing and Third Parties

Information may be processed by third-party services when you choose to use their integrations. Their own privacy policies apply.

We do not sell personal information.

7. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures are intended to protect information handled by the application.

Information should be retained only as long as necessary for the relevant feature or purpose. Temporary event data is intended to remain limited in memory.

No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

SYNTXRR STREAM is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

9. Your Choices

You can manage Android permissions, unlink supported accounts where available, and remove locally stored application data through the available application or Android settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application develops. The latest version will be published at this page.
